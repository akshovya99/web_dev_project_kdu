About the Department

Welcome to the Smart Computing Department at Kyungdong University. Our program, taught entirely in English at the Global Campus, is dedicated to shaping the next generation of tech innovators. We provide a robust curriculum focused on high-demand fields such as Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cyber Security, and IoT.

Degree Requirements

  • Core Courses: 90 credits
  • Elective Courses: 30 credits
  • General Education: 10 credits
  • Minimum GPA: 2.5
  • Completion of Capstone Project & Internship

Facilities

Our Smart Computing Department offers state-of-the-art laboratories and learning spaces that support hands-on exploration, research, and innovation.

AI and Machine Learning Lab

Equipped with high-performance computers and GPUs to support research and practical training in artificial intelligence, data science, and machine learning.

Software Development Lab

Provides an environment for full-stack development, team projects, and coding competitions with modern development tools and cloud resources.

Robotics Lab

Designed for robotic system design and automation experiments, integrating sensors, actuators, and embedded control systems.

IoT and Systems Lab

Focuses on connected systems, embedded programming, and smart environments using IoT devices and real-time monitoring setups.
